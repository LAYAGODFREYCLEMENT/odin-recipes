Deep fried Chicken Recipe

Indulge in the ultimate deep-fried chicken, featuring a shatteringly crisp exterior and an incredibly juicy, tender interior.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Buttermilk
  • Hot sauce

Steps for making deep fried Chicken

  1. Pat Dry:Remove the chicken from its packaging and pat each piece completely dry with paper towels. This helps the marinade cling directly to the meat
  2. Combine Liquids: In a large mixing bowl or a heavy-duty, resealable plastic bag, whisk together the buttermilk and hot sauce.
  3. Add Base Seasoning: Whisk in a pinch of salt, black pepper, and garlic powder directly into the liquid. Seasoning the liquid ensures the flavor travels into the meat, not just the crust.
  4. Submerge: Place the chicken pieces into the liquid, ensuring every piece is completely submerged and coated.
  5. Refrigerate:Seal the container or bag and place it in the refrigerator.
  6. Timing is Key:Marinate for a minimum of 2 hours, but overnight (8 to 12 hours) is highly recommended for the most tender results. Do not exceed 24 hours, or the acid can make the meat mushy.
