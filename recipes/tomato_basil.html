One-Pot Tomato Basil Rigatoni

A rich, comforting pasta dish where everything cooks in a single pot. The starch from the pasta cooks directly into the crushed tomatoes and broth, creating a velvety, thick sauce without any heavy cream.

Ingredients

  • pasta
  • Aromatics
  • Liquid & Tomatoes
  • Seasoning
  • Finishers

Preparations steps for making One-Pot Tomato Basil Rigatoni

  1. Sauté aromatics:Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Sauté the onion and garlic for 3 minutes until softened.
  2. Combine everything:Add the uncooked pasta, crushed tomatoes, broth, oregano, red pepper flakes, salt, and pepper to the pot. Stir well to combine.
  3. Bring to a boil: Turn the heat up to high and bring the liquid to a rolling boil
  4. Simmer and stir:4. Reduce the heat to medium-low so it maintains a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered for 11–13 minutes, stirring frequently to keep the pasta from sticking, until the pasta is al dente and the liquid thickens into a rich sauce.
  5. Melt and garnish:1. Remove the pot from the heat. Stir in the fresh basil and cheese, allowing them to melt completely into the hot pasta before serving.
